Stylized Wooden Buckets, Tubs & Kegs — 11 Hand-Painted Props

11 game-ready wooden containers — buckets, tubs, vats and kegs — hand-painted stylized look, 3 LODs, convex colliders, 2K textures, URP materials. 1.2–1.5k tris each.

What's inside

  • 3 buckets: classic, sturdy, tall
  • 3 tubs: wide, shallow, heavy
  • 3 vats: classic, big, straight
  • 2 kegs: small, tall
  • 22 prefabs: each prop as a lit PBR version and as an Unlit version (baked colour, for mobile and stylized lighting)
  • Demo scene with all props

Every prop is modelled from real stave-and-hoop construction: individual planks, iron hoops, the main straps with lifting ears. The shapes read from a distance and hold up close.

Technical

  • Triangles per prop: LOD0 1,156–1,532, LOD1 1,084–1,444, LOD2 708–972
  • LODGroup on every prefab (3 levels), convex mesh collider (48 tris)
  • Textures per prop, 2048×2048: Base Color, Normal (OpenGL), Metallic-Smoothness, baked Unlit colour
  • 1 material per prop, 2 draw calls per LOD
  • Real-world scale, pivot at the bottom centre, Y up
  • No n-gons, no open edges, UVs in 0–1 without overlaps
  • URP only (URP Lit and Unlit). Made and tested in Unity 6000.2.6f2 with URP 17.2
  • No scripts, no third-party dependencies
